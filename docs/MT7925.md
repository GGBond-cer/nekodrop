# MediaTek MT7925 (`mtradio --mt7925`) Bring-Up & Findings

This note documents the pure user-mode MediaTek MT7925 (`PCI\VEN_14C3&DEV_7925`, Connac3) AirDrop and 5 GHz Concurrent Wi-Fi implementation in `mtradio/`, hardware findings, and live benchmarks on Windows 11.

## Verified Hardware & Protocol Findings (`SUBSYS_60001A3B`, Windows 11 + iPad)

1. **PLE Block Ack Window Cap (`kMt7925BaUnicastSlots = 60` < `64`)**:
   - Allowing 128 unicast frames in flight against a 64-frame negotiated BA window wedged the Connac3 PLE per-WCID queue once sequence numbers advanced past the active BA window across AWDL channel switches.
   - Capping per-WCID BA inflight frames to `60` with an `8 ms` pre-hop TX drain eliminates mid-transfer stalls, and `recover_stalled_peers()` (`sta_remove` + token reclaim + lossless requeue + `sta_add`) recovers any transient WCID wedge in ~48 ms with zero packet loss.
2. **Firmware Rate Control (`STA_REC_RA`) Low-Rate Mask on 80 MHz**:
   - Unmasked `STA_REC_RA` initialized peer rates at `OFDM 6 Mbps` / `HT MCS 0`, capping the first ~40 MB of a transfer to ~11 MB/s.
   - Masking `OFDM 6–18 Mbps` and `HT MCS 0–3` (`supp_ht_mcs = 0xf0`) on 80 MHz AWDL peers (matching `iwxradio`) lets firmware rate adaptation ramp straight into VHT80 2SS (`MCS 7–9`).
3. **Why Peer `STA_REC` Uses VHT80 2SS TX + Optional `--he80` RX A-MSDU**:
   - Attaching `STA_REC_HE_V2` (`802.11ax` TX) to an unassociated `kConnectionInfraAp` monitor BSS on MT7925 triggers `fw-event: eid 00` after ~48 channel switches. Keeping peer `STA_REC` TX on VHT80 2SS (matching `iwxradio`) avoids firmware TX stalls across arbitrary hops, while `--he80` enables `awdld -H -M 1500` and `amsdu = 1` in `ADDBA Response` + `STA_REC_BA` (accelerating both incoming files and reverse HE80 A-MSDU TCP ACKs during outbound transfers).
4. **Windows 11 Service Power Throttling**:
   - Disabling `PROCESS_POWER_THROTTLING_EXECUTION_SPEED | PROCESS_POWER_THROTTLING_IGNORE_TIMER_RESOLUTION` on `nekodrop-radio-mt.exe --mt7925` prevents Windows 11 from coalescing 1 ms sleep ticks to 15.6 ms inside a background service session.

## Live Throughput Summary (Windows 11 x64 ↔ iPad)

| Direction & Mode | Channel Topology | Payload | Result |
|---|---|---|---|

## Scope & Unverified Boundaries

- **`14C3:0717` (AMD RZ717)**: kept informational (`mtk`) until verified on RZ717 hardware.
- **2.4 GHz (`StationOn24GHz: false`)**: MT7925 runs AWDL on 5 GHz 80 MHz (`149` / `44`) and joins 5 GHz Wi-Fi networks only in Concurrent mode.
- **Strict IOMMU remapping (`IOVA != PA`)** and **forced `TerminateProcess` mid-DMA**: tested with Windows 11 VBS + HVCI enabled and AWE physical pages `< 4 GiB` across normal service stop/restart and supervised rollback; strict IOMMU address remapping and hard `TerminateProcess` fuzzing mid-DMA have not been separately tested.
